### Interview caveats that must remain accurate

- The 0.48% metric is **mean 12-month forward proxy return** with monthly overlapping formations; **do not label as CAGR** or live portfolio performance.
- Full yield/slope spanning leaves only 0.229% alpha (NW t=1.52, n=243) — not a proof of significant independent alpha.
- No actual FX-forward P&L, sovereign-credit hedge or institutional implementation has been delivered.
- The 10-country configuration produced **9 usable markets** and **6 active legs**.
